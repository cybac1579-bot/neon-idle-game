## 2026-11-14 — Confirmed: permanent deep-zone dungeon and separate research economy
- User chose **B: core upgrades + a separate permanent research catalog**, NOT a large catalog of interchangeable core multipliers. Existing seven core upgrades are provisional, not the approved long-term catalog. The research catalog must spend a **distinct dungeon-only currency**, separate from ordinary rebirth cores and higher-rebirth currencies. Research should be eventually fully maxable, not permanently mutually exclusive.
- Working name **심층 구역** (not final). The dungeon is a **lightly interactive choice-based** experience layered over automatic combat. Occasional choices may change rewards, risk, next encounters, or research unlocks; do not require a choice every floor or repetitive tapping.
- **Floor persistence is permanent across every rebirth tier:** once floor N is cleared, the next entry starts at **N+1**, including after leaving, defeat, app restart, ordinary rebirth, and higher rebirth. Never force replay of dozens/hundreds of cleared floors. First-clear rewards must not be farmable by replaying.
- **Crucial correction:** dungeon combat MUST use the player's **current ordinary combat strength**, NOT independent permanent dungeon combat stats. After rebirth, the next uncleared floor may be too difficult; that is deliberate. Entry itself can remain possible, but success requires rebuilding ordinary strength. This prevents endlessly clearing the dungeon in one isolated loop and ties it back to the main progression. Do not auto-scale enemies down to current strength or allow dungeon-only power to bypass the intended gate.
- Research progress, highest cleared floor, and dungeon-only currency persist through **all** rebirth tiers, even if upper-tier rebirth resets lower-tier investments. Future upper tiers may reset their own subordinate layers, but this dungeon is the explicitly permanent anchor.
- **Balance philosophy:** do not equalize every choice's efficiency. Reward good strategy, risk and occasional high-value outcomes. Strong combinations are allowed; rigorously reject only extreme acceleration that trivializes rebirth/main combat or makes all other systems irrelevant. Evaluate relative efficiency, total progression pace, and repeatability separately.
- User approved design discussion, **not** implementation of a dungeon UI or final reward numbers. Before calling the dungeon playable, build a small end-to-end slice with durable save/restore, choice consequence, power gate, single-claim floor rewards, and reset regression tests; then evaluate in browser. No merge to production main until verified.
- Design advantages (importance): durable long-term objective (very high), meaningful occasional agency (high), connection to main progression (very high). Risks: permanent bonus snowball (very high), interaction fatigue if too frequent (high), save/reward duplication across resets (high). Mitigate with one-time floor rewards, sparse choices, current-power gate, and regression tests rather than artificial equalization of all strategies.
